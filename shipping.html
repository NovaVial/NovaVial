Shipping Policy

NovaVial Research ships research-use materials to eligible addresses after order review and payment confirmation. Shipping timelines, carrier availability, and packaging requirements may vary by destination and product handling needs.

Processing

Orders are reviewed before fulfillment. We may contact customers to confirm business, institutional, or research-use details before shipping.

Delivery

Tracking details are provided when available. Customers are responsible for entering an accurate delivery address and receiving packages promptly.

Restrictions

Products are offered for research use only and are not for human or veterinary use. Orders may be canceled when legal, carrier, payment processor, or compliance requirements cannot be satisfied.
