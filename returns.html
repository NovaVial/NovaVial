Return Policy

Due to the nature of research-use materials, opened, used, temperature-sensitive, or otherwise compromised items are not eligible for return. Contact support promptly if an order arrives damaged or incorrect.

Refund Review

Refunds are reviewed case by case. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through the payment processor.

Order Issues

Customers should retain packaging, product labels, and delivery records when reporting a damaged or incorrect shipment.
